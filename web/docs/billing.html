Plans and billing

Plans are measured in builds, because a build is the only thing here that costs real machine time. Everything else - editing, previewing, downloading what you already built - is unlimited.

The plans

PlanBuilds a monthPrice
Starter25UGX 19,000
Business100UGX 57,000

Both cover as many apps as you like. The allowance is builds, not apps, so an agency running six client apps that each ship rarely may well fit in Starter.

25 builds is more than it sounds

Remember that content changes need no build at all - your app loads your live site. You build when the app itself changes, which for most people is a burst during setup and then occasionally.

Paying

Mobile money. Open Billing, choose a plan, enter the number to charge, and press pay. A prompt appears on that handset; enter your PIN and the plan activates.

There is no card form and no redirect to another site. You never leave Web2App, and no card details exist anywhere in this product.

What the screen is doing while you wait

Mobile money has no way to tell us the moment you approve, so the server asks the network repeatedly until it has an answer. That is why the screen waits rather than jumping straight to a result.

You can close the tab

The server keeps chasing a payment on its own. Approve the prompt after shutting your laptop and the plan still activates. It survives a restart of the server too - every payment is written to disk before the network is even called, so nothing is only in memory.

If the prompt never arrives

Check the number, and check the line has mobile money active. An unanswered prompt is marked abandoned rather than failed, because nothing was charged - you can simply start again.

If you were charged but nothing activated

Rare, and recoverable. Every payment has a reference kept from before the network was contacted, so there is always a thread back to the money. Get in touch with the number and the approximate time.

Anything the server cannot interpret - a timeout, a dropped connection, an answer it does not recognise - is treated as still pending, never as failure. Reading an unclear answer as failure is how a system keeps somebody's money and gives them nothing.

When the allowance runs out

Builds stop. Your apps, settings and previously built artifacts stay exactly where they are - you can still download anything you built earlier, and your published apps are entirely unaffected. They are on Google Play, not here.

What happens to your work

Your published apps belong to you, signed with your key, on your developer account, loading your website. Nothing about them depends on this service continuing to exist. If you stop paying, or we stop existing, your apps go on working and your notifications go on arriving.

You can also download the complete Flutter project at any time, on any plan. It is a standard project that any Flutter developer can build. There is no lock-in to be trapped by.
