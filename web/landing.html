Frequently asked questions

The short honest answers, before you spend a build finding out.

How do I build an app without writing code? +
Enter your website URL, give the app a name and an icon, and press Build. The server generates the app, builds it, and gives you the files to download. Nothing to install on your computer.
Can I publish to Google Play? +
Yes. Upload your keystore and each build comes out as a signed .aab, which is exactly what the Play Console takes. Your keystore passwords are entered per build and never stored. You publish it on your own Play account.
What about the App Store? +
Apple's build tools only run on macOS, so we give you the complete iOS project as a .zip instead - with your bundle ID, name, icons and permission text already in place. Open it on a Mac, run flutter build ipa, and submit from there.
Does the app stay up to date with my website? +
Yes - the app shows your live website, so anything you change on the site appears in the app immediately. You only rebuild when you want to change the app itself: its name, icon, or features.
How do payments work? +
Choose a plan and enter your mobile money number. A prompt appears on your phone; you approve it with your PIN there - never on the website. The plan switches on as soon as the payment lands, even if you have closed the page.
What do I need to sign up? +
A phone number - we text you a code to confirm it. You start with 3 free builds and no card is ever asked for.
